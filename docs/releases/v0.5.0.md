Windows installer: download `GeoLock_0.5.0_x64-setup.exe` and run it (no admin needed). It updates 0.4.x in place and keeps your library, settings and models.

**New in 0.5.0: more accurate on its own**

- **Looks at more of the photo.** Besides the four views it used before, GeoLock now also looks at each quarter of the photo zoomed in, so signs, poles and plants are seen in more detail. This adds about 0.07 s per photo on the Arc GPU.
- **Re-tuned on real photos.** How much GeoLock trusts the scene (vegetation, architecture, road style) and its country guess was fitted on half of a 294-photo Wikimedia Commons test set. On the other half, which it never saw, the typical (median) error dropped from 785 km to 636 km. The right country went from 33 % to 36 %, and every distance from 25 km up gained 2 to 3 points.
- **Reads licence plates and postcodes.** A plate in a known national format (about 30 countries) or a postcode now counts as a clue. Postcodes are looked up in GeoNames' free postcode list (included, about 10 MB) to pin the town. A plain number counts only when the town's name is next to it, as in "75008 Paris". It's rare on test photos, but very strong when it fires.

One trade-off: GeoLock now calls more photos "over 50 % sure", and those are right (within 250 km) about 69 % of the time, against 81 % before.

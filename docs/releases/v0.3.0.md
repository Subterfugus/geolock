Windows installer: download `GeoLock_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe`, run it (no admin needed) and open GeoLock from the Start menu. On first launch it downloads the CLIP model once (about 600 MB).

**New in 0.3.0**

- **Smarter second opinion.** When you ask Claude or ChatGPT, they now work through a 12-point geoguessing checklist (driving side, road lines, signs, bollards, plates, poles, script, architecture, plants, terrain, sun). They also get zoomed crops of the signs GeoLock read, and they give a reason for each guess plus the places they ruled out. The app shows all of this. The analyst stays off until you tick it.
- **Folders and export.** Drop or open a whole folder (Ctrl+Shift+O) and GeoLock works through it with a progress bar. You can export one photo's map as a PNG, or the library as CSV, KML or JSON. When a photo has its own GPS, the app shows how far GeoLock's guess was from the real spot.
- **Laptop speed mode.** CLIP runs on the GPU, NPU or CPU and falls back to the next device if one fails. Settings > Processor shows where it runs and how fast, and it has a speed test.

Windows installer: download `GeoLock_0.4.1_x64-setup.exe` and run it (no admin needed). It updates 0.4.0 in place and keeps your library and models.

**New in 0.4.1**

- **The second opinion now checks its evidence online.** Claude and ChatGPT were told not to use any tools, so they answered from memory. Now they can search the web, and the prompt tells them how: look up the exact shop names, street names, phone numbers and web addresses they read, check that their best guess really has that road, town or chain, and take coordinates from what they confirmed. A sign with a business name can now pin a photo to one street instead of one country.
- You can see the searches while it works ("Searching the web: …" and a count), and the answer card lists what it checked under **Checked online**.
- It's on by default. Turn it off in Settings › Second opinion › **Let them search the web** for a faster, photo-only opinion. Only words are searched, never the photo. Searching makes an answer take longer, so the time limit is 3 minutes longer when it's on.

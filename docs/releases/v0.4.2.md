Windows installer: download `GeoLock_0.4.2_x64-setup.exe` and run it (no admin needed). It updates 0.4.x in place and keeps your library and models.

**New in 0.4.2**

- **Analyst pins land on the right town.** Claude and ChatGPT often name the right town but give coordinates that are tens of km off. GeoLock now looks the town up in its own list of 140,000 places and moves the pin there. The answer card says when it did ("Moved 42 km onto …"). A pin already close to the town it names stays where it is, so a precise street location isn't pulled to the town centre.
- **Country odds.** Analysts now say how likely each country is, separately from their pinned guesses. GeoLock uses those odds for the part of their answer they didn't pin down, instead of falling back on its own country guess.
- **Close-ups of the road.** For big photos (12 MP phone shots and up), the analysts also get two full-size close-ups of the road area, so they can read licence plates, bollards, poles and road lines that are too small in the whole photo. This adds roughly 5,000 image tokens to each ask.

Windows installer: download `GeoLock_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe` and run it (no admin needed). It updates 0.3.x in place and keeps your library and models.

**New in 0.4.0**

- **Second opinion you can see and steer.** Ask Claude and Ask ChatGPT now come with a model picker and a reasoning-effort picker. The lists come from your installed `claude` and `codex` tools, and your choice is remembered. While the analyst works you see each step, a timer, the line it's thinking about right now, and a Stop button. Stopping keeps the result the photo already had.
- **Street-level map.** The map now opens on a street map (OpenStreetMap, free, no key) with roads, buildings, places and landmarks, and you can zoom all the way in. Satellite now shows road and place names on top of the imagery. Without internet GeoLock falls back to the offline map, and if you zoom in on that it offers to switch to streets.
- **Settings are easy to find.** There's a labeled Settings button in the library and a gear at the top. Settings has a sidebar with Processor, Analysis, Second opinion, Map, Appearance, Library, Shortcuts and About.
- **New look.** Warm graphite (or paper in light mode), one orange accent, square corners, IBM Plex type and monospaced numbers. There's a new app icon to match.
